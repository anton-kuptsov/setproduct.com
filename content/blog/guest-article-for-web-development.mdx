<span class="blog\_big-paragraph">Every web development company is always looking for ways to increase the domain’s ranking, attract more traffic and ensure a constant influx of leads.</span>

Due to a large number of companies, who are competing for attention, it can be problematic to stand out. The greatest method to achieve the above mentioned goals - guest posting. However, a lot of companies are struggling with crafting a convincing draft to submit.

## The Efficiency of Guest Posting

Creating backlinks, increasing your brand’s credibility, and strengthening SEO metrics overall -  the reasons why your web company should use guest posting. Unfortunately, the majority of web development companies haphazardly approach the placement of guest posts. Thus, a loss of time and declined pitches - the only result that they have.

If you want to avoid this “result” - target audience and define your goals. And to set them - ask the following questions: the main reason for guest posting? increasing online visibility or establishing a lead funnel to pump revenue? target audience? the most popular websites that your audience visits?

Answers to these questions will allow you to create a powerful strategy.

## Identifying Guest Posting Opportunities

To find the ideal match for your guest posting efforts can be discouraging. Nevertheless, there are a few tips to identify opportunities that align with your aims and target audience. The first one - the usage of online tools (Ahrefs, Moz, or SEMrush). They will help you in finding websites with high domain authority and relevance to your specific niche.

The second one - researching industry-specific websites, blogs, and publications aimed at your target audience (with "**Write for Us**" or "**Guest Post**" section). Additionally, you can search for websites that used to publish guest posts - they are probably open for future submissions. For a full walkthrough, see [how to find guest posting opportunities](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/guest-posting-opportunities).

Let’s consider 5 key characteristics to follow while identifying guest posting opportunities:

-   _Relevance_: does the website have the appropriate level of relevance to your niche and target audience?
-   _Authority_: does the website possess a strong reputation and high domain authority?
-   _Audience engagement_: does the website have an active audience? (evidenced by comments, social shares, and total traffic).
-   _Content quality_: does the website have high-quality,informative and well-written content?
-   _Guest posting guidelines_: Is it convenient to follow the website's guest posting guidelines?

Using online platforms and directories is also helpful for finding out guest posting opportunities. The popular ones: [Smashing Magazine](https://www.smashingmagazine.com/), [A List Apart](https://alistapart.com/), [Web Designer Depot](https://www.webdesignerdepot.com/), [TheDesignest](https://thedesignest.net/)

## Get Your Guest Posts Accepted Faster

![Get your guest posts accepted faster](/blog/assets/guest-article-for-web-development/img-1.webp "float-right")

Creating a compelling draft plays a crucial role in getting guest posts accepted faster. In order to reach that - research the website, its terms and adapt your draft. 

Firstly, read the [website's guidelines](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/submit-a-guest-post) and existing guest posts to find everything out. Secondly, make a draft that outlines your topic, the relevance to the website's audience and future benefits to the website. Then, include a brief bio to highlight your expertise and credentials. And finally, post a link to your website or social media profiles.

There are some tips for your perfect and quickly accepted draft:

1.  **Comprehend the Website's Requirements**: Pay attention to the guidelines and the preferred word count, tone and style of writing.
2.  **Write a Compelling Title**: Create an attention-grabbing title that reveals the main topic of your draft. Don’t forget about its relevance to the website’s niche.
3.  **Create a Strong Introduction**: Write a concise and understandable introduction that will be capable of explaining the purpose of your draft.
4.  **Use a Simple Writing Style**: Nothing should confuse the reader. Thus, use a clear writing style without long and complex sentences or technical terms. 
5.  **Optimize Your Draft for SEO**: Include relevant keywords, phrases and use a keyword research tool.
6.  **Include a Brief Bio**: At the very bottom of your draft leave a brief bio that will highlight your expertise and credentials. Don’t forget about your website and social profiles’ links.

Now let’s take a look at some do's and don'ts to bear in mind while creating the draft:

### DO:

-   _Personalize your approach_: Address the reviewer or editor by name. 
-   _Show your expertise_: Pay attention to your relevant skills and experience and explain the relation between them and your topic.
-   _Outline your topic_: Explain the proposed topic in your draft and highlight its relevance to the audience of the website. 

### DON'T:

-   _Plagiarize or duplicate content_: Avoid copying from other resources. Otherwise, plagiarism can damage your reputation and lead to rejection.
-   _Use AI-generated text without humanizing it_: Review and edit the content to avoid detection by GPT-checkers.
-   _Forget to proofread_: Check the text carefully to avoid mistakes that can cause a negative impression.

## Guest post topics that work for web development companies

Editors accept pitches that teach their readers something specific. For a web development company, your day-to-day work is a goldmine of angles no generic marketer can write. These topics consistently earn placements:

-   **A Core Web Vitals case study.** Show real before/after numbers from a site you optimized — LCP, INP, and CLS with the exact fixes. Concrete performance data is rare and highly shareable.
-   **A headless CMS or framework migration.** Walk through moving a client from a monolith to a headless or Jamstack setup: trade-offs, gotchas, and results. Editors love decision-making stories.
-   **A design-to-code workflow.** Explain how your team ships from Figma to production — tokens, components, handoff. This bridges design and dev audiences and pairs naturally with UI kit topics.
-   **An accessibility audit walkthrough.** Document how you found and fixed WCAG issues on a real project. Accessibility content ranks well and signals genuine expertise.
-   **A performance budget in practice.** Describe how you set and enforced a budget across a team — tooling, CI checks, and the impact on load times.
-   **A framework comparison from real projects.** Compare React, Vue, Svelte, or Astro based on what you actually shipped, not benchmarks alone. First-hand comparisons cut through the noise.

Pick the angle where you have proof — screenshots, metrics, or code. That evidence is what separates an accepted draft from a generic one.

## A pitch template for web development teams

Keep the pitch short, specific, and easy to say yes to. Lead with a relevant result, then offer a few topic options:

```text
Subject: Guest post pitch — [specific topic] for [site name]

Hi [editor's name],

I lead development at [company], where we recently [specific result —
e.g. "cut a client's LCP from 4.1s to 1.3s ahead of a Black Friday launch"].
I read your piece on [related article] and think your readers would get
value from a hands-on follow-up.

Three angles I could write, each with real project data and screenshots:

1. [Topic option 1 — e.g. "How we shipped a Core Web Vitals fix in one sprint"]
2. [Topic option 2 — e.g. "Design-to-code: our Figma-to-React handoff"]
3. [Topic option 3 — e.g. "Migrating a legacy CMS to headless without downtime"]

I'll match your tone and word count, and everything is original and
proofread. Here's a recent sample: [link].

Happy to adjust to what fits your calendar.

Thanks,
[Name] — [company], [portfolio link]
```

Personalize every field. A pitch that references the editor’s own content and offers concrete, data-backed topics gets replies far more often than a generic template.

## Frequently asked questions

**What should a web development company write guest posts about?**
Write about the work you actually do: performance optimization, framework migrations, design-to-code workflows, accessibility, and real project case studies. First-hand technical content with data is what editors accept and readers trust.

**Where can web development companies publish guest posts?**
Target relevant, authoritative design and development publications — Smashing Magazine, A List Apart, and niche blogs your clients read — plus platforms like Setproduct that reach founders, designers, and developers. Prioritize relevance and real traffic over raw domain rating.

**How long should a web development guest post be?**
Follow each site’s guidelines, but most technical guest posts land between 1,200 and 2,000 words. Depth matters more than length — a focused case study with code and metrics beats a padded overview.

**Do I need to be a strong writer to guest post?**
You need clear thinking more than polished prose. Outline your topic, lead with the result, and keep sentences simple. Editors will help refine wording, but they can’t supply your first-hand expertise.

**How soon will guest posting bring leads?**
Referral traffic can arrive the day a post goes live, but SEO and lead compounding take months of consistent, relevant placements. Treat guest posting as a steady program, not a one-off campaign.
